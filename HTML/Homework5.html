Inspirational Quotes

"Small steps make big progress."

This quote reminds me that even small efforts can lead to great results. Consistency is the key to success.

"Believe in yourself."

It means you have to trust your abilities and never give up, even when it is hard. Self-belief helps you go further.

"A better tomorrow starts today."

This quote encourages me to make good choices today. It reminds me that every day is a new opportunity to be better.
